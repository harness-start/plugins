# video-shotcraft attribution

The bundled offline shot catalog, recipe text, and normalized source snapshots are derived from [Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft](https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft) at commit `0d6f0b57f0d4d6700761644c07f7ef03c3e50234`, licensed under Apache-2.0.

Normalization replaces external static assets with a synthetic local placeholder, freezes three browser-layout measurements for deterministic rendering, and adds attribution comments. Upstream audio files and other binary assets are not bundled.
