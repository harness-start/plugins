# Accessibility

For each manifest slide, declare a meaningful title, logical reading order, alt-text obligations for informative visuals, and at least one non-color information channel. Use concise on-slide labels instead of depending on a legend when practical.

Review keyboard-independent reading as a fixed document: title first, then the primary claim, evidence, and qualification. Avoid overlapping objects, clipped text, tiny footnotes, and text embedded in raster images when native text is possible.

Automated checks cover signatures, page mapping, declared reading order, non-color encoding, and role contrast. They do not prove semantic accuracy of alt text, correct natural-language reading order, or comprehension. The independent review must cover those remaining judgments and identify its evidence source as a tool report, measurement, content review, or manual walkthrough.
