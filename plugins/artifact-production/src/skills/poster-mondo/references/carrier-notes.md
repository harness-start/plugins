# Carrier notes

## Event poster

Prioritize title, date, venue, and action in that order. Verify information from the expected viewing distance and reserve texture for non-critical regions.

## Book cover

Keep title and author as separate semantic roles. Test the front cover alone and as a small catalogue thumbnail. Do not let symbolic novelty make the title ambiguous.

## Album cover

Test at square thumbnail size. One recognizable silhouette or type relationship usually survives better than several small narrative objects.

## Social card

Use the declared platform ratio. Keep essential copy inside the carrier safe area and test the crop that appears in feeds or previews.

## Prompt package

When advice will be translated into an image prompt, preserve the structured fields: subject, composition, letterform dimensions, palette, material, copy policy, and negative rules. A prompt is production input, not evidence that the output satisfies the brief.
