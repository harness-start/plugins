# Assets and accessibility

Every retained asset records path, SHA-256, role and source type. Generated assets also record tool, model and prompt digest. Licensed or public-domain assets record source URL and rights status. Unknown rights are not releasable.

Final typography is deterministic. AI imagery may contain no required factual text. Check contrast against declared semantic pairs, body and caption minimums, safe area, non-color encoding, thumbnail readability, exact Chinese glyphs and punctuation, and absence of accidental remote or private data.
