# Quality gates

- Source: complete brief, one profile, exact copy, local assets, ordered variants/layers, pure deterministic layer modules.
- Render: self-contained SVG, current source-hash proofs, exact pixel dimensions, decoded nonblank PNG, source-bound render evidence.
- Probe: independent SVG→PNG byte equality, contrast and typography measurements, safe-area and accessibility evidence.
- Review: full-size and thumbnail inspection of hierarchy, typography, legibility, clipping, color, copy, profile fidelity and asset integrity.
- Release: current independent review, exact SVG/PNG manifest, digest-bound receipt and no unresolved high/critical finding.

Regional review also checks non-substitutable cultural evidence. Mondo checks original symbolic compression rather than artist imitation. Editorial checks brief-driven reading rhythm. Academic checks takeaway and figure scanability.
