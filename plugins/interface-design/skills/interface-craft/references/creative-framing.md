# Creative framing

Use this only for a material redesign, when the current directions are generic or stuck, or when the user explicitly requests alternatives. Routine fixes and faithful extensions skip or bypass it.

## Choose exactly one method

- **assumption inversion**: invert one load-bearing convention, then keep only consequences that still serve the job;
- **concept fan**: step from the requested solution to its purpose, then branch into different mechanisms for that purpose;
- **mechanism analogy**: borrow how another domain coordinates attention, state, or movement, not its surface style;
- **SCAMPER**: transform an existing coherent concept when the problem is variation rather than direction;
- **bounded stimulus**: introduce one relevant constraint, material, content pattern, or interaction primitive when the space is empty.

Do not chain methods or decide by majority vote. Name why the chosen method fits the blockage.

## Diverge

Produce no more than three mechanism-distinct directions. For each, state the mechanism, observable design consequences, cost, risk, and fastest validation. Differences in accent color, font, or decoration do not count. Show any duplicate, forced, or dead-end route and why it was abandoned instead of silently replacing it.

## Converge

Compare the live directions against the user's job, brand evidence, one-sentence retell target, and semantically causal signature cue. Recommend one only when the evidence distinguishes it; otherwise ask one selection question. Record the chosen direction through the existing design brief or design memory, not a new schema.
